## About

**JUSIAI GAMES** is a one-person game studio. Game design, art, and programming are all done by one developer.

The brand began with a small aquarium. Our mascot is a yellow guppy swimming through lime-green water plants. Even in a small tank, a guppy never stops swimming. We want to work the same way: make small things, show them often, and keep moving forward.

## How we work

- **Make it small.** We make games small enough for one person to finish.
- **Show it often.** We share the work in progress on our devlog, not just the finished game.
- **Keep moving.** We would rather swim a little every day than try one big leap.
